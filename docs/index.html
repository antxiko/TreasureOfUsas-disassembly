What turned up when we took it apart

Mood changes the game

Each hero has four states: happy, calm, sad and angry (p06:A644). A happy Wit jumps a second time in the air, once per jump (p01:64F1); a happy Cles keeps walking on thin air for 32 frames as long as the direction is held (p01:62C7).

Read in the code.

One shot and one blow per state

Wit's shot is type 4 minus the state: waves, bar, ball or beam (p01:6B0D); Cles's blow is the state plus 6 (p09:B7C9). An angry Cles hits hard and breaks the big crate (p01:7398, p01:7431). Happy, nobody pushes blocks; the other three states push them, each at its own speed (p01:7BD0).

The 喜楽哀怒 items

Each one sets its mood (p01:75EE), and each ruin sets the starting one (p00:4575).

Read in the code.

The pause is a shop

In INFORMATION you buy SPD and JMP with coins (p06:A0EA). The prices depend on the character: Wit 70 and 60, Cles 50 and 80 (p06:BF6C).

Read in the code.

Other Konami cartridges

The game looks in the other slots for four catalogue numbers (p06:B751). With RC-749 (The Maze of Galious) you start with 100 coins (p00:45F7); with RC-750 (Metal Gear), half the damage (p01:72FF); with RC-751 (Nemesis 2), F5 CONTINUE after GAME OVER (p00:4427); with RC-752 (F1 Spirit), the happy powers, always (p01:64F1, p01:62C7).

Read in the code.

Two languages in one cartridge

A Japanese MSX gets the texts in Japanese; any other, in English (0x002B, p05:9849). The font holds hiragana and 28 kanji (p14:9289).

Read in the code.

The passwords are the names of the ruins

On reaching ruins 1-4, a map makes the ruin blink and types out its password: JUBA RUINS, HARAPPA RUINS, GANDHARA RUINS or MOHENJO DARO (p06:A2BB). On the title screen, CTRL or button B asks for it (KEYWORD, p00:4643), and with it you start at that ruin (p00:45CB).
