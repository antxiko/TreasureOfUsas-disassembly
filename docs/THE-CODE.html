The code

The figures

  • 100 % of the binary explained: 41,458 bytes of traced code and 89,614 of named data, adding up to the cartridge's 131,072.
  • 20,434 instructions in banks 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 13 and 15, with 8,185 comments: 40.1 %.
  • 2,536 routines and labels; none below 10 % comments, and not one call to an unnamed routine.

How it is organised

The game's frame (p00:40E0) dispatches on the game state (0xC000, 14 states, table p00:40F4): start-up, the wait, the demo, PLAY START, the scenes, play, on dying, GAME OVER, the change of screen, the partner captured, the end of the ruin, INFORMATION (0x0B and 0x0D) and the password (0x0C).

The records

IX points to four kinds of record, and tools/anotaciones.py comments their fields wherever IX is certainly one of them:

  • actor (0xC400 and 0xCD00): the type, the step, Y, X, the speeds and accelerations, the frame, the counter, the life and what it collides with;
  • character (0xC2B0 and 0xC2D0): whether they have fallen, the speed and jump levels, the life and the mood;
  • shot (0xC320): the type, Y, X, the speeds, the direction and its sprite;
  • sound channel (0xC01C...): the track, the tempo, the volume, the octave, the instrument and the envelope.

The actors

There are 63 types at 0xC400 (handlers p02:82E9), placed screen by screen by p09:B12B. Their pictures are built from pieces (p15:B744) with their own palette (p09:B781). The traps at 0xCD00 are another list, of 13 types (p15:A4F9), with RLE patterns (p00:4926).

The texts

The font starts with 0x2B = "A" and holds hiragana (0x49-0x76) and 28 kanji (0xA0-0xBB, p14:9289). There are three formats: caption, typewriter (FD breaks the line, FE hands the word to the other one) and plain.

The names

The names and the automatic comments come from tools/anotaciones.py, each with the routine where it is read; tools/anota.py writes them into the notes.

The Treasure of Usas was published by Konami for the MSX2 in 1987; its catalogue number is RC-753 and it is 128 KB. All rights in the game remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
