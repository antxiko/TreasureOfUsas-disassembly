The music

Everything you hear here comes from running the cartridge's own sound engine (bank 13, p13:6000) on an emulated Z80, frame by frame at 60 per second, just as the interrupt calls it (p00:4048). What the engine writes to the PSG is turned into sound with an AY-3-8910 (tools/musica.py). Each MP3 is the whole piece up to where it starts again, plus 8 seconds of the repeat fading out.

Checked against openMSX

The PSG writes have been compared frame by frame with those of openMSX (Japanese C-BIOS MSX2). In every frame the PSG ends up the same:

  • the whole antechamber tune (1,454 frames);
  • the ruin 4 tune at pitch 0x80, the whole piece (6,614 frames);
  • the start of the game tune (178 frames).

To hear the ruin 4 tune without playing up to it, the probe swapped the antechamber request for that tune (tools/omsx_psg.tcl). The timbre is not checked: it is a textbook AY-3-8910, not a recording of the emulator.

Each ruin's tune changes with the mood

On entering a stage, musica_de_fase (p00:54C8) asks for the ruin's tune (p15:BE70). It also stores a pitch in 0xC122, p15:BE7A[ruin × 4 + state], which depends on the mood of whoever goes in. With it the engine shifts the note table (p13:625D) and, from instrument 2 on, the instrument (p13:62E2):

Happy is always 0x20 and calm always 0x00. Sad and angry share 0x80 and 0x40: in Pegu and Alchi, sad is 0x80 and angry 0x40; in Dunhuang, Hunza and Agra, the other way round.

F5 changes a voice in Dunhuang

During a stage, F5 silences the music (p00:5FEF) and brings it back (p00:5FE6). When it comes back it is taken from another table, p15:BE75, which only differs in Dunhuang: you get 0x85, the same score with a different second voice (p13:7690 instead of p13:7507).

The rest

The antechamber: 0x89 (p00:424C).

At the start of a game: 0x8A (p00:41DB).

The statue room: 0x86 (p00:5512).

The bosses: 0x87 in the statue room (p02:8D75). 0x88 plays in each stage's boss room (p02:90A1) and is the same piece: the same three pointers in p13:672C.

On finishing a ruin: 0x8B (p00:454E).

On finishing the last one, Agra: 0x8C (p00:4552).

Death: 0x8D (p01:6309).

GAME OVER: 0x8E (p00:440A).

0x8F is silent: its three voices begin with the end-of-track code (0xFF).
